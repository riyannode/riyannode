### About me

I like building systems where AI agents can do more than just generate text.

Most of my work sits around the layer between **an agent deciding to do something** and **value actually moving onchain** — payments, wallets, identity, permissions, receipts, trust, and risk.

I prefer keeping financial actions behind **deterministic controls**, while using LLMs where they actually make sense: planning, reasoning, discovery, and coordination.

### What I care about

* Agents that can **actually transact**, not just talk.
* Financial actions protected by **deterministic policy boundaries**.
* Wallet infrastructure that does not expose raw private keys to agents.
* Payments with receipts, traceability, and enough context to audit later.
* Open-source primitives other builders can actually reuse.
* Shipping systems that work end-to-end instead of staying as demos.
